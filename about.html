Our story

We build digital presence that reflects the value of what you offer.

Baiden Creatives is an organisation based in East Legon, Accra. Its creative services build business websites, online stores, booking systems and custom web applications, and design the flyers, social graphics and brand materials that go with them.

Baiden Creatives also owns BAID X, a platform that connects skilled professionals with businesses and project teams. Each organisation under Baiden Creatives has its own brand and focus. See the structure
