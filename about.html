Our story

We build digital presence that reflects the value of what you offer.

Baiden Creatives is a web and graphic design studio in East Legon, Accra. We started with a simple idea: every business deserves a website and a brand that look as good as the work behind them.

Today we build business websites, online stores, booking systems and custom web applications, and design the flyers, social graphics and brand materials that go with them.
