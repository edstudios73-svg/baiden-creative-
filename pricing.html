FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?+

Projects under GH₵10,000 are paid 60% upfront and 40% before launch. Larger projects are paid 50% upfront, 30% during development and 20% before final deployment.

What is not included in the package price?+

Domain and hosting renewals, premium plugins, paid APIs, stock images, professional photography, copywriting, large-scale data entry, third-party subscriptions, payment gateway charges, and major changes outside the agreed scope.

What happens when my website launches?+

We handle the full launch: domain and hosting setup, SSL/HTTPS, mobile and tablet optimisation, final testing and deployment. Business and Professional packages also include Google Analytics, Google Search Console, an XML sitemap and search indexing setup.

What do I need to provide to get started?+

Your logo, images, business information, contact details, social media links and any content you already have. If you do not have these yet, we will guide you through it.

Where is Baiden Creatives based, and do you work with clients outside Accra?+

Baiden Creatives is based in East Legon, Accra, Ghana. We work with businesses, organisations and individuals across Ghana, and projects can be delivered fully remotely.
