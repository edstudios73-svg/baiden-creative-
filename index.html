Who we are

An organisation building digital products and brands for Ghana.

Baiden Creatives was founded by Baiden David and Baiden Daniel in East Legon, Accra. It delivers creative services to businesses and organisations, and it owns BAID X, a workforce platform that sits under it as its own organisation.
